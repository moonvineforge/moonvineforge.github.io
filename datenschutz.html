Website Statistics with GoatCounter

This website uses GoatCounter to measure visits and to display a public total visitor count. GoatCounter does not use cookies, local storage or persistent tracking identifiers in visitors' browsers.

GoatCounter processes aggregated website statistics such as visited pages, referrers, browser and operating-system information, approximate location, language and screen width. IP addresses and full user-agent strings are not stored in the database. They may be held temporarily in memory for up to eight hours in order to distinguish repeated visits during a session.

The use of GoatCounter is based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used and in improving its content. GoatCounter.com is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland. According to GoatCounter, service data is stored on servers operated by Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany.

Further information is available in the GoatCounter privacy policy .
