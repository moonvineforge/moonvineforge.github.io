# Card Forge Taxonomy

This document describes the broad idea categories used by the Community Card Forge.

## Card

A direct player action drawn and played during combat.

## Relic

A passive item that modifies rules, rewards patterns, or opens build paths.

## Enemy Action

A move or pattern used by an enemy during combat.

## Status

A temporary or persistent modifier attached to a player or enemy.

## Resource

A tracked value that can be gained, spent, transformed, or checked by effects.

## Mechanic

A broader rule idea that may later become part of cards, relics, enemies, or encounters.
