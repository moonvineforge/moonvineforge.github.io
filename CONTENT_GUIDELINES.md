# Content Guidelines

Moonvine Forge content should be clear, compact, and useful for visitors.

## Voice

- Use direct, readable English.
- Keep fantasy flavor secondary to clarity.
- Prefer concrete mechanics over vague promises.
- Avoid overexplaining small features.

## Examples

Example cards, relics, enemies, statuses, and resources should show a clear mechanical idea.

A good example usually includes:

- a short name
- a type
- a simple effect
- a visible player choice or tradeoff
