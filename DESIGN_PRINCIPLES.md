# Design Principles

Moonvine Forge favors mechanics that are modular, readable, and easy to combine.

## Principles

1. Start simple.
2. Make the choice visible.
3. Prefer reusable effects over one-off exceptions.
4. Keep numbers adjustable.
5. Let theme support the mechanic, not replace it.

## Good mechanic seeds

Strong mechanic ideas often involve:

- timing
- risk and reward
- delayed payoff
- target choice
- resource conversion
- interaction with statuses or relics
