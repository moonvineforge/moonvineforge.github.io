How the forge works

Systems first. Personality always.

The goal is not to produce interchangeable deckbuilders. It is to build flexible foundations that still leave room for unusual mechanics, memorable worlds, and meaningful player experiments.

01

Modular by design

Cards, statuses, resources, triggers, and targets are assembled from reusable systems instead of being locked into one game.

02

Deterministic at the core

Seeded runs and explicit effect resolution make complex interactions reproducible, testable, and easier to understand.

03

Built for strange ideas

Community concepts are not decoration. They are architecture tests that reveal where the engine must become more flexible.
