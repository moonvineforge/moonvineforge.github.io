Community design experiment

Community Card Forge

Design a card, ability, item, enemy move or strange little game mechanic for a roguelike-inspired system.

This is a small open design experiment by Moonvine Forge Studios. The goal is to understand what kinds of effects, costs, drawbacks, synergies and balance problems players naturally imagine when they are allowed to build their own content.

Submit a design View the core engine
01

Build freely

Your idea does not have to follow one specific game. It can be a card, passive ability, item, relic, spell, enemy action, curse, status effect or any other turn-based roguelike mechanic.

02

Think in systems

You can define costs, resources, drawbacks, numbers, targets, scaling, rarity, intended power level and the kind of build the design should support.

03

Help with balance

Submissions may be used as design and balancing data for a modular roguelike deckbuilding core engine. Weird, risky and broken ideas are useful too.

Submit your idea

Forge a mechanic

Text-only submissions are enough. No artwork is required. If you leave a Nickname, Reddit username or other alias, it will only be shown publicly if you allow credit in the form.

What happens next?

From community ideas to balancing data

Once enough designs have been submitted, the results can be grouped by cost type, effect type, intended power level, drawback style and balance risk. The long-term goal is to use these patterns to inform a flexible engine where players and designers can create content without hardcoding every single effect.
