Need a spark?

Begin with a design problem, not an empty form.

Generate a prompt, twist it into something personal, or ignore it completely. The goal is to get from “I have no idea” to one mechanic worth explaining.

Current design prompt

Design a relic that remembers the order in which cards changed zones, then rewards one specific sequence.

Use this as a starting point →

A strong submission can include

  • A trigger: when, why, or how it activates.
  • An effect: what changes when it resolves.
  • A target: who or what it can affect.
  • A cost: energy, health, cards, time, or risk.
  • A purpose: which strategy it should support.
  • An edge case: the strange interaction you hope works.
