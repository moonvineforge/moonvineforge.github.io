A strong submission can include

  • A trigger: when, why, or how it activates.
  • An effect: what changes when it resolves.
  • A target: who or what it can affect.
  • A cost: energy, health, cards, time, or risk.
  • A purpose: which strategy it should support.
  • An edge case: the strange interaction you hope works.

Design pillars

What makes a strong Moonvine idea?

Readable first

A good idea should be understandable before it becomes clever. Strange mechanics work best when the first sentence is clear.

Interesting choices

The strongest cards ask players to decide between timing, risk, resources, and long-term consequences.

Composable effects

Ideas that combine simple native effects are easier to test, balance, and reuse across enemies, relics, and events.

Submission tips

How to make your idea easier to use

  • Give the idea a short name that hints at its mechanic.
  • Describe what happens in one clear sentence before adding flavor.
  • Include rough numbers only if they matter to the concept.
  • Explain the intended tension: risk, reward, timing, scaling, or tradeoff.
